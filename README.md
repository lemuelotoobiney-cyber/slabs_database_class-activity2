Systems Analyst for SuperMart SA
----------------------------------

1.) Transaction Processing System (TPS):
    There needs to be new knoweledge on the customers purchases and
    inventoty stocks records.

2.) Management Information Systems (MIS) & 
    Decision Support Systems (DSS):
    The mangement will coduct an automated feedback analysis for
    roles and responsibility for the business through performance tracking,
    making business decisions, plans and workflow during the sudden
    drop in sales.This checks revenue, sales figures or inventory.
    In that way SuperMart SA management can choose to
    run a promotion.

3.) Executive Support Systems (ESS):
    They need a telecommunication, computing and information 
    displaying  reports, graphs, and along with monitoring
    performances, tracking comptitors stragies and predict future trends.

4.) Knowledge Work Systems (KWS) & Office Automation (OAS):
    A tool used in a KWS is computer-aided design systems(CAD) to 
    automate their design process like stocking up shelves and
    store layout.
    OAS example: Google Teams used by SuperMart SA’s Head staff to 
    manage emails, schedule meetings, and organize tasks.

IT Landscape & Legacy Systems
-----------------------------

SuperMart SA has grown over 15 years and now uses a mix of old 
legacy software and modern cloud apps.

- Connecting to old Legacy systems to modern systems is complex because
  the data structures, information are very different and it might require   more hardware for the system to work properly.

- EOL(End-of-life) assest tracking prevents disinvestments like old 
  computers that have old systems like windowes 7.SuperMart SA can 
  replace items before the breakdown.
  Standardization of IT systems will help lower cost  of the IT landcape
  by having one main program to document and contain information of all the
  jobs in SuperMart  SA.
